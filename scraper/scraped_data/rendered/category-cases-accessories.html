Storage cases and vial organizers

A peptide storage case protects lyophilized and reconstituted reference-material vials from light and impact and keeps a research collection organized and lot-traceable. We offer single- and multi-vial cases in several finishes, plus organizers and lab consumables.

Cases and accessories are non-hazardous lab supplies; they ship without the cold-chain and research-use restrictions that apply to the compounds themselves.

Frequently asked questions

What is a peptide storage case?
A peptide storage case is a protective organizer for research-peptide vials — it shields vials from light and impact and keeps a laboratory collection organized and lot-traceable. We offer single- and multi-vial cases in several finishes.
Do your cases fit standard peptide vials?
Yes. Our cases are sized for standard research-peptide vials; each product page lists the case capacity and dimensions.
How should research peptides be stored?
Lyophilized peptides are generally kept cold and protected from light in a labeled, organized container until reconstitution. A dedicated storage case helps maintain organization and lot traceability across a collection.
