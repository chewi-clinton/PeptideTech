What Is BPC-157?

BPC-157 (Body Protection Compound-157) is a synthetic pentadecapeptide corresponding to a partial sequence identified within human gastric juice, extensively studied in preclinical models of tissue repair, angiogenesis, and gut-brain axis signaling.

Research Background

Published rodent and in-vitro studies have examined BPC-157's interaction with growth-factor signaling pathways (including VEGFR2), nitric-oxide system modulation, and fibroblast migration assays relevant to wound-healing research models.
