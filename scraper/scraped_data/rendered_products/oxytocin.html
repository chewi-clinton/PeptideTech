What Is Oxytocin?

Oxytocin is a cyclic nonapeptide hormone studied extensively as an agonist of the oxytocin receptor (OXTR), a class A GPCR, in neuroendocrinology and social-behavior research models.

Research Background

Oxytocin research examines OXTR-mediated calcium signaling and downstream gene-expression pathways in both peripheral (uterine, mammary) and central-nervous-system cell and animal models.
