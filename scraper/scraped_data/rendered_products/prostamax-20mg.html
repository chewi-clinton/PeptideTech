What Is Prostamax?

Prostamax (Lys-Glu-Asp-Pro) is a short synthetic tetrapeptide bioregulator from the Khavinson research series, studied in prostate-tissue gene-expression research.

Research Background

This bioregulator class is examined for tissue-specific transcriptional effects, with Prostamax research concentrated on prostate-derived cell models and cellular differentiation pathway studies.
