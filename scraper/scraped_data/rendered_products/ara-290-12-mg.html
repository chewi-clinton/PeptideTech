Overview

ARA-290 (Cibinetide) 12 mg is an 11-amino-acid peptide derived from the helix-B region of erythropoietin (EPO), supplied by Peptide Tech at ≥ 99% purity (HPLC/MS verified) for in-vitro, cellular, and biochemical research use only (RUO). ARA-290 (also referred to as pHBSP) is frequently used to probe EPOR/βc (CD131) signaling and cell-protective response pathways in controlled laboratory models.
